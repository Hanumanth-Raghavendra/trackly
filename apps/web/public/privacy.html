Trackly

Privacy policy

Last updated 29 October 2026. Applies to the Trackly website and Android app.

Trackly is a personal, open-source portfolio project. It stores only what it needs to work, does not show ads, does not sell or share your data, and has no third-party analytics or tracking.

What Trackly stores

DataWhy
Email address, display name, a salted hash of your password (never the password itself) To create your account and sign you in
Your timezone and daily focus goalSo days, streaks and reminders match your clock
Tasks, projects, habits and check-ins (with optional mood and notes), goals, focus sessions That's the app: the lists, charts and coach notes are computed from them
Session tokens and the device's browser/app name To keep you signed in and to detect stolen sessions
Notification subscriptions (only if you turn notifications on)To send the reminders you asked for

Where it's kept

Data is stored in a PostgreSQL database (Neon) and processed by the Trackly servers (Render), both in Singapore. The website is served by Vercel. Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). In the Android app, your sign-in token is kept in the app's private storage and excluded from device backups.

Optional AI features

If the server has an AI provider turned on and you use Break it down or AI summary, Trackly sends that task's title and description, or that week's totals, to the AI provider to generate the text. Your name and email are never sent.

Demo accounts

"Try the live demo" creates a temporary account filled with sample data. It is deleted automatically, with everything in it, after 24 hours.

Your control

  • Export: Settings → Your data → Download my data gives you everything as JSON.
  • Delete: Settings → Your data → Delete account permanently removes your account and all its data from both services.
  • Notifications: turn them off any time in Settings or in your device settings.
